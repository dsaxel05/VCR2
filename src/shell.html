ICON:ledger

Revenue rebuilt from the ledger

Drop in revenue by customer by month. VCR2 builds the MRR bridge, true cohort retention, concentration and cohort heatmaps: two days of associate work, in a second.

ICON:compare

Stated against computed

If the deck says 134% NRR and the cohorts say 99%, that gap is the finding. A binomial test tells you when every error happens to flatter the company.

ICON:forensics

Forensics on the billing data

Pre-raise booking spikes, one-off charges booked as recurring, duplicate customers, quarter-end pull-forward and a Benford test, each with its method and its limits.

ICON:odds

Runway odds, not a runway number

A seeded Monte Carlo gives the chance of reaching next-stage metrics before the cash runs out, and how much capital that takes at 80% confidence.

ICON:deal

Deal maths to the exit

Pool shuffle, SAFE and note conversion, a term-sheet review against market standard, VC Method and First Chicago returns, and a full liquidation waterfall.

ICON:library

Learns from your deals

Save runs and import comparables to see percentiles against your own deal flow, and recalibrate the healthy ranges on it.

Also:

VCR2 is a workflow tool. It does not give investment advice and its score is not a recommendation to buy or sell a security. Both examples are fictional companies with synthetic data.
