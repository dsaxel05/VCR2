Revenue rebuilt from the ledger
Drop in revenue by customer by month. Radar builds the MRR bridge, true cohort retention, concentration and cohort heatmaps — two days of associate work, in a second.

Stated vs. computed
If the deck says 134% NRR and the cohorts say 104%, that gap is the finding. A binomial test tells you when every error happens to flatter the company.
🔬
Forensics on the billing data
Pre-raise booking spikes, one-off charges booked as recurring, duplicate logos, quarter-end pull-forward and a Benford test — each with its method and its limits.
🎲
Runway odds, not a runway number
A seeded Monte Carlo gives the probability of reaching next-stage metrics before the cash runs out, and how much capital that takes at 80% confidence.
💼
Deal maths to the exit
Pool shuffle, SAFE and note conversion, a term-sheet review against market standard, VC Method and First Chicago returns, and a full liquidation waterfall.

Learns from your deals
Save runs, import comparables, or paste anything and let AI structure it. Percentiles against your own data — and a rubric you can recalibrate on it.

Radar is a workflow tool. It does not give investment advice and its score is not a recommendation to buy or sell a security. Both examples are fictional companies with synthetic data.
